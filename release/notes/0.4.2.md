# Runx CLI 0.4.2

Runx 0.4.2 finishes the invocation cutover and raises the quality bar for
first-party skill content.

## Changed

- Skill invocation runs through one path, and receipts carry flat execution
  identity fields.
- The thread and knowledge model was cut over in place, and the official
  release surface was reduced to what the registry serves.
- Skill quality profiles are first-class, and first-party proposal quality is
  part of the core authoring contract.

## Removed

- The Sourcey adoption scan and preview lanes: they belong to Sourcey, and the
  generic `sourcey` skill stays.

## Fixed

- The `sourcey` skill extracts rendered content from a docs shell.

## Notes

Published to npm on 2026-04-23.
